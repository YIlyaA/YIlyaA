# Image ideas

Briefs for pictures the owner generates. Each brief says what the picture has to do and what cannot change. The look is the creator's choice.

## fox-scene

- Used in: the top of `README.md`, the only animation on the profile.
- File to drop in: one looping video, `fox-loop.mp4` (or several clips in order: `fox-1.mp4`, `fox-2.mp4`, ...). A still of the fox alone, `fox-sheet.png`, helps but is optional. The README assets (`assets/fox-scene.webp`, `assets/fox-scene-still.png`) are built from the video.
- Size and aspect: 21:9 if the tool offers it, otherwise 16:9 with all the action inside the middle band (the frame is cropped to about 21:9). 1080p or higher. 10 to 14 seconds in total.
- Job: a visitor sees in a few seconds how the owner works. Small robot agents do the work, he watches over them, and when one fails he fixes it himself and the work goes on. It should feel calm and a little funny, not busy.
- Fixed:
  - The fox is an original character that stands in for the owner: light sandy fur (he is blond), blue eyes. Not Nick Wilde and not any other existing film or game character.
  - The fox sits at a laptop with a cup of coffee. Small robots walk past him in a line and carry blocks.
  - The story, in this order:
    1. The fox drinks coffee. The robots walk past with their blocks.
    2. One robot falls and drops its block.
    3. Every robot stops and looks at the fallen one. The fox turns to look too.
    4. The fox types hard for about two seconds, then hits Enter with one big visible move.
    5. The fallen robot gets up and all the robots walk on.
    6. The fox looks again, sees it is fine and picks the coffee back up.
  - The loop is seamless: the last frame equals the first one. With several clips, each clip starts on the frame where the previous one ended.
  - The camera does not move, zoom or cut.
  - No text, logos, watermarks or readable screen content anywhere in the frame.
  - The picture sits on a white page (`#ffffff`) and on a dark one (`#0d1117`) as a framed strip with rounded corners, so it needs its own background. Transparency is not needed.
  - One orange accent close to `#FF6B00` somewhere in the scene, because the contact button on the page has this colour.
- Open: style and medium (3D, 2D or mixed), the look of the fox beyond the fixed features, what he wears, the robots' design, what the blocks are, the place, time of day, light, colour treatment, composition.
- Alt text: "A fox at a laptop drinks coffee while small robots carry blocks past him. One robot falls, the others stop, the fox types a fix and the line moves on."
